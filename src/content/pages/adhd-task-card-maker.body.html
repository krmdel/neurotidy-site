    Four rules for a card that actually gets used

    1. It lives where the task happens. Dish card by the sink, shower card on the mirror. A card in a drawer is a card that does not exist, which is object permanence at work.
    2. The first step takes ten seconds. Music on, water running, shoes on. Starting is the hard part, so make it nearly free.
    3. Steps are actions you can see. "Wipe the counter you use most", not "tidy the kitchen".
    4. The bad-day line is a full win. Write the version you could do on your worst day, and count it.
