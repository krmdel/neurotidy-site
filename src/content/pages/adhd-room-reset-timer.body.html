Why a reset works when "clean the room" does not

"Clean the room" is not a task. It is a pile of hidden decisions: where to start, what counts, when you are done. An ADHD brain tends to stall at exactly that point, which is why wanting to clean and being able to start can feel like two different circuits. A reset removes the decisions. Each step is one visible action you can start in under ten seconds, and the timer tells you when to move on.

The five steps always run in the same order: clear what does not belong, bin the trash, restore one thing, clear one surface, and finish with the step that makes the room feel different. More tools like this are on the free ADHD tools page.
