ADHD cleaning planner: one week, one 10-minute reset a day

neurotidy.co

How it works. Each day has one theme, one named first step, and a bad-day line. Set a 10-minute timer, do the first step, keep going until it rings, then stop even mid-task. On a day you cannot function, do only the shaded line and tick the box. It counts.

This week's one big thing (only one)

The 1-3-5 idea: one big task a week is enough. Everything else on this page is small on purpose.
